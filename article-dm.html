Data Mesh: when centralizing data is no longer the answer

Data Mesh is less about technology and more about changing who owns data.

The problem

The traditional centralized Data Warehouse model can work well, but as organizations grow it can become a bottleneck: every new request, definition and quality issue is routed through one central team. Data Mesh proposes a different operating model.

My approach

The four principles are domain ownership, data as a product, self-service infrastructure and federated governance. The important question is not only how data is processed, but who is accountable for its reliability and meaning.

What this means in practice

In a BI environment with ERP, operational, commercial and production sources, this distinction matters. A dashboard can expose a problem, but sustainable data quality requires ownership close to the business domain.

For BI leaders, the practical lesson is simple: centralize what should be standardized, but do not centralize every responsibility. Good architecture makes ownership visible.
