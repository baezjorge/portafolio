Why Most BI Projects Fail Before the First Dashboard Is Built

The biggest BI risks often appear before a single visual is designed.

The problem

When requirements are vague, stakeholders disagree about definitions, or nobody owns the final decision, the project can produce a technically correct dashboard that solves the wrong problem.

My approach

Before building, I would align four things: the business question, the decision owner, the KPI definitions and the expected outcome. Then validate the available data against those requirements. This prevents the common situation where the team discovers late that a critical metric has no reliable source or that different departments use different definitions.

What this means in practice

The next step is to prototype with stakeholders. A small validated prototype creates a much faster feedback loop than building a complete report and waiting for approval at the end.

BI is a business change project supported by technology. Requirements alignment is therefore not bureaucracy; it is risk management.
